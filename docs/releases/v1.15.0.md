# OpenRepro-Agent v1.15.0

OpenRepro-Agent v1.15.0 adds reviewer packets for human claim evidence review.

## Added

- Added `openrepro reviewer-packet`.
- Added `reports/reviewer_packet.json`, `reports/reviewer_packet.md`, and optional `reports/reviewer_packet.zip`.
- Summarized claim evidence, signoffs, validations, open actions, review order, and source artifact hashes.
- Surfaced reviewer packet status in `inspect`, `status`, reports, handoff, and evidence packages.

## Policy

Reviewer packets organize workflow review evidence for humans. They do not prove scientific reproduction.
