# OpenRepro-Agent v1.48.0

v1.48.0 adds promotion and release gates for engineering artifact state.

## Added

- `openrepro promote plan/record/summary`.
- `workspace/promotion_plan.json` and `workspace/PROMOTION_PLAN.md`.
- `workspace/promotion_record.json` and `workspace/PROMOTION_RECORD.md`.
- `workspace/promotion_registry.json` and `workspace/PROMOTION_REGISTRY.md`.

## Behavior

- Promotion targets are `experiment`, `report`, and `delivery`.
- Promotion states include `validated`, `accepted`, `released`, and `rejected`.
- Plans evaluate target-specific required gates and optional warnings.
- Records require explicit `--confirm` and passing gates before registry writes.
- Local UI, dashboards, evidence explorers, and evidence packages surface promotion artifacts when present.

## Guardrail

Promotion gates manage workflow release state only. They do not claim scientific reproduction or independently validate formulas, data, code, or results.
