# OpenRepro-Agent v1.13.1

OpenRepro-Agent v1.13.1 adds reviewer-facing claim evidence reports.

## Added

- Added `openrepro claim-evidence-report`.
- Added `reports/claim_evidence_report.json` and `reports/claim_evidence_report.md`.
- Combined claim binder evidence, binder validation, and latest claim signoffs per claim.
- Surfaced claim evidence report status in `inspect`, `status`, reports, handoff, and evidence packages.

## Policy

Claim evidence reports summarize workflow evidence and human signoffs. They do not prove scientific reproduction.
