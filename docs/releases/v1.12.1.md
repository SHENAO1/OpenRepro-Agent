# OpenRepro-Agent v1.12.1

OpenRepro-Agent v1.12.1 adds claim evidence binder validation.

## Added

- Added `openrepro validate-evidence-binder`.
- Added `workspace/claim_evidence_binder_validation.json` and `workspace/CLAIM_EVIDENCE_BINDER_VALIDATION.md`.
- Detected stale binders when claims, coverage, review decisions, data, specs, or runs change.
- Surfaced binder validation status in `inspect`, `status`, reports, handoff, and evidence packages.

## Policy

Binder validation checks workflow evidence freshness and internal consistency only. It does not prove scientific reproduction.
