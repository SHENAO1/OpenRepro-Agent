# OpenRepro-Agent v1.31.0

OpenRepro-Agent v1.31.0 adds a static paper evidence explorer for reviewer-facing navigation.

## Added

- `openrepro evidence-explorer`
- `reports/evidence_explorer/index.html`
- `reports/evidence_explorer_manifest.json`
- Optional `reports/evidence_explorer.zip`

## Changed

- `refresh` now regenerates the evidence explorer after paper lineage.
- The workflow registry includes `evidence_explorer` after paper lineage.
- Static dashboards link to the evidence explorer when present.
- Evidence explorer outputs are excluded from evidence-package freshness fingerprints.

## Guardrails

- Evidence explorers organize workflow evidence only.
- They do not verify scientific correctness.
- They do not infer missing claims or fabricate results.
- They do not claim scientific reproduction success.
