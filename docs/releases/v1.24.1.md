# OpenRepro-Agent v1.24.1

OpenRepro-Agent v1.24.1 adds per-agent task dispatch packs.

## Added

- `openrepro agent-dispatch`
- `workspace/agent_dispatch.json`
- `workspace/AGENT_DISPATCH.md`
- `workspace/agents/<agent>/TASKS.md`

## Changed

- `refresh` now regenerates the dispatch pack after the agent board.
- `status`, `inspect`, reports, and handoff files now surface dispatch pack status.
- Evidence-package freshness excludes generated dispatch artifacts because they are downstream coordination outputs.

## Guardrails

- Dispatch packs are advisory handoff files.
- They do not launch agents.
- They do not execute task commands.
- They do not run experiments.
- They do not close human review decisions.
- They do not add claim signoffs.
