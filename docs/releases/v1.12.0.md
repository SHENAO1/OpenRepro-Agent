# OpenRepro-Agent v1.12.0

OpenRepro-Agent v1.12.0 adds claim evidence binders.

## Added

- Added `openrepro evidence-binder`.
- Added `workspace/claim_evidence_binder.json` and `workspace/CLAIM_EVIDENCE_BINDER.md`.
- Bound traced claims to experiments, runs, registered data, quality gates, protocol coverage, and review decisions.
- Surfaced incomplete claim counts in `inspect`, `status`, reports, handoff, and evidence packages.

## Policy

Claim evidence binders organize workflow evidence only. A complete binder is not a scientific reproduction claim.
