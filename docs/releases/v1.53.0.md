# OpenRepro-Agent v1.53.0

v1.53.0 adds OpenRepro-Bench Lite, a built-in curated suite for repeatable workflow-compliance checks.

## Added

- `openrepro bench-lite`.
- `--list-tasks`, `--task-id`, `--project-prefix`, `--bench-dir`, and `--materialize-only` options.
- BOC-like, random-search, and numeric-table toy note tasks.
- Materialized suite files under `benchmarks/openrepro_bench_lite/`.
- `openrepro_bench_lite_summary.json` and `OPENREPRO_BENCH_LITE_SUMMARY.md`.

## Policy

OpenRepro-Bench Lite delegates to the existing benchmark runner and reports workflow-compliance evidence only. It does not claim paper reproduction success or scientific benchmark scores.
