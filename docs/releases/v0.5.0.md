# OpenRepro-Agent v0.5.0

OpenRepro-Agent v0.5.0 starts the stronger paper-to-code and repair loop with
human candidate approvals and controlled dry-run repair previews.

## Added

- Added `openrepro approve-candidates` to promote selected formula and parameter
  candidates into `workspace/verified_candidates.json`.
- Added `workspace/VERIFIED_CANDIDATES.md` for human-readable approval notes.
- Updated experiment scaffolds to detect verified candidates and mark scaffolds
  as `verified_inputs_ready`.
- Added `openrepro repair --dry-run` with `workspace/repair_dry_run.json` and
  `workspace/REPAIR_DRY_RUN.md`.
- Added manifest regeneration diffs for dry-run previews when manifests are
  missing or no longer match files on disk.

## Policy

Verified candidates are implementation inputs only. Repair dry-runs do not
mutate files, fabricate missing scientific artifacts, or claim paper
reproduction success.
