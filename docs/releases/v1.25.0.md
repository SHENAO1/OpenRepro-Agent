# OpenRepro-Agent v1.25.0

OpenRepro-Agent v1.25.0 adds safe agent execution dry-run plans.

## Added

- `openrepro agent-exec-plan --dry-run`
- `workspace/agent_exec_plan.json`
- `workspace/AGENT_EXEC_PLAN.md`

## Changed

- `refresh` now regenerates the safe execution dry-run plan after the dispatch pack.
- `status`, `inspect`, reports, and handoff files now surface execution-plan status.
- Evidence-package freshness excludes generated execution-plan artifacts because they are downstream coordination outputs.

## Guardrails

- Execution plans are dry-run only.
- They do not execute commands.
- They do not run experiments or reruns.
- They do not close human review decisions.
- They do not add claim signoffs.
- They do not apply repairs.
- Placeholder and human-input tasks remain blocked.
