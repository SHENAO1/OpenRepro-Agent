# OpenRepro-Agent v1.34.0

v1.34.0 adds goal-oriented workflow preset plans over the registered DAG.

## Added

- `openrepro workflow preset`.
- `workspace/workflow_preset.json` and `workspace/WORKFLOW_PRESET.md`.
- Presets for `data`, `review`, `delivery`, `agent`, and `full` workflow views.
- Preset status summaries, runnable counts, blocked/stale counts, and next-command guidance.

## Changed

- Refresh now writes the default `delivery` workflow preset after evidence query.
- The workflow DAG includes `workflow_preset` as a derived delivery step.

## Guardrails

- Presets are planning views only.
- They do not execute experiments, ingest sources/data, record human decisions, or apply repairs.
