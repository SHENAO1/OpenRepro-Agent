# OpenRepro-Agent v1.1.0

OpenRepro-Agent v1.1.0 improves paper evidence extraction and review triage.

## Added

- Added section-aware provenance for formula and parameter candidates.
- Added `workspace/caption_index.json`.
- Added `workspace/CAPTION_INDEX.md`.
- Added candidate `risk_level` and `risk_flags`.
- Surfaced high-risk candidate counts in `openrepro inspect`.
- Included caption index artifacts in evidence packages.

## Policy

Sections, captions, and risk flags are review aids. They do not verify formulas,
parameters, or scientific correctness.
