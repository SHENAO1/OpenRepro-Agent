# OpenRepro-Agent v0.7.1

OpenRepro-Agent v0.7.1 adds a candidate review lifecycle for formula and
parameter candidates.

## Added

- Added `openrepro list-candidates`.
- Added `openrepro review-candidates`.
- Added review statuses: `verified_by_human`, `rejected_by_human`, and
  `needs_more_evidence`.
- Added `workspace/candidate_reviews.json` and
  `workspace/CANDIDATE_REVIEWS.md`.
- Synced `verified_by_human` reviews into `workspace/verified_candidates.json`.

## Policy

Candidate review status records human workflow decisions only. It does not
claim formulas, parameters, experiments, or papers have been reproduced.
