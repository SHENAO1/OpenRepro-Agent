# OpenRepro-Agent v1.13.0

OpenRepro-Agent v1.13.0 adds human claim signoffs for evidence binder records.

## Added

- Added `openrepro claim-signoff`.
- Added `workspace/claim_signoffs.json` and `workspace/CLAIM_SIGNOFFS.md`.
- Tracked signed, unsigned, accepted, rejected, deferred, and open claim counts.
- Surfaced claim signoff status in `inspect`, `status`, reports, handoff, and evidence packages.

## Policy

Claim signoffs record human workflow decisions about evidence records. They do not prove scientific reproduction.
